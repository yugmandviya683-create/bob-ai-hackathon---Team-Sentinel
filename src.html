The Problem

Protocol deviations go undetected until it's too late

A single rejected FDA submission cascades into catastrophic financial and patient safety consequences.

  • 🚫
    Missed Patient Visits

    No system flags when scheduled visits are skipped across hundreds of dispersed sites.

  • 💊
    Wrong Dosing Events

    Manual review misses dosing errors hidden in thousands of unstructured clinical records.

  • ⚠️
    Banned Co-Medications

    Concomitant medications prohibited by protocol go undetected until audit time.

  • 📋
    Late ICF Execution

    Informed consent forms signed after study procedures compromise GCP compliance.

  • 🏥
    No Site-Level Visibility

    Risk managers lack leading indicators to identify high-risk sites before problems escalate.

Deviation Classification

ICH E6 GCP Severity Framework

Every deviation is automatically tagged using the internationally recognised GCP severity hierarchy.

Deviation Type Severity GCP Reference Risk Impact Auto-Mitigation
Late or missing Informed Consent Major ICH E6 R2 §4.8 Patient safety + data integrity Immediate site notification + re-consent protocol
Banned co-medication administered Major ICH E6 R2 §4.5 Efficacy confound + safety signal CAPA + protocol amendment review
Dosing outside protocol window Major ICH E6 R2 §5.18 PK/PD data invalidation risk Dose documentation audit + sponsor escalation
Missed scheduled visit (≥7 days) Minor ICH E6 R2 §4.9 Data completeness Reschedule trigger + patient retention alert
Lab sample outside processing window Minor ICH E6 R2 §8.3 Analytical validity Re-draw recommendation + lab notification
Missing source document signature Admin ICH E6 R2 §8.2 Documentation compliance Automated reminder to site coordinator
Late eCRF data entry (≥3 days) Admin ICH E6 R2 §5.5 Data timeliness Escalating email reminders to site staff

Deviation: Banned co-medication (Warfarin) administered to Patient P-2847 at Site S-042
Severity: Major
Root Cause: Site pharmacist unaware of protocol exclusion criteria update (Amendment 3).
Corrective Action: Immediate re-training of all site pharmacy staff on Amendment 3 within 5 business days.
